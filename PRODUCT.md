# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A local-first personal and household expenditure tracker, not a budgeting tool. Its mechanism separates purchase expenditure, household classification, funding source and reimbursement settlement.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Four fixed sources: Nationwide personal current, joint current, personal credit card and American Express personal card. CSV previews and mapping, reviewable local merchant suggestions, split household shares, named payday periods, purchase-linked refunds and allocated manual reimbursements. Data stays in browser storage with JSON backup/restore. No credentials, cloud deployment, external AI processing, budgets or automated payments.

## Evidence on Hand
Only fictional fixtures and a separate fictional demo. No real bank CSV sample has been supplied. Supported bank layouts must not be described as real-file verified.

## Product Principles
Uncertain transactions remain visible; totals distinguish confirmed spending from unresolved movements. Merchant corrections are one-off unless explicitly remembered. Joint-paid purchases never create personal reimbursement entitlement. Transfers never count as purchase expenditure; reimbursement linking never deducts twice.

## Open Decisions
The Commonplace display name and task-first ledger presentation are implementation choices, not user-pinned branding. Local-first dependency-free browser implementation follows the approved brief.
